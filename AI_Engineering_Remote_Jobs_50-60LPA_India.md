## ⚠️ Important Caveats

- **50-60L at 4-6 YOE = Senior IC (L5/L6 equivalent)** — need strong system design + ML depth
- **"Remote" varies by team** — confirm with hiring manager: *"Is this role fully remote indefinitely for India-based employees, or hybrid?"*
- **Verified remote for India**: Atlassian ("Bengaluru or Remote"), Databricks ("Remote India"), GitLab ("Remote, India"), ServiceNow (WFH reviews), Snowflake (remote roles), Datadog (remote India), Hasura (fully remote)
- **Hybrid/confirm per role**: Postman, Razorpay, BrowserStack (culture flags)
- **Equity is major differentiator** — Atlassian, Databricks, Snowflake, Postman RSUs can push total comp >70L
- **Notice period**: Most Indian companies require 60-90 days — factor into timeline
- **Culture due diligence**: Check Blind, Glassdoor, AmbitionBox reviews for BrowserStack, Turing, Postman before committing
